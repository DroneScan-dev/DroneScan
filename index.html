About the project

Messy field footage, made structured.

DroneScan organizes drone footage into something easier to read, while the drone is still flying. The goal is simple: take raw footage, keep objects tracked, show their movement direction, mark important areas, and surface changes in real time.

DETECT

Recognize

Turn drone footage directly into structured objects, not just shapes in a video.

TRACKING

Follow

Monitor movement through trails, clusters, and zones so activity stays connected as conditions change.

ACTION

Respond

Surface what needs attention right away, so operators focus on decisions instead of rewatching footage.
